Homing

Reconnect Earbuds · macOS

Your AirPods hop between devices. Homing brings them back to your Mac with one click in the menu bar or a global shortcut, and can switch audio output and microphone to them as soon as they connect.

macOS 13+Menu barSandboxedOpen source · MITFree

GasBlender

Nitrox & Trimix planning · macOS · iPhone · iPad

A planning tool for trained gas blenders: nitrox and trimix fills with oxygen, helium, air and optional premix, fill sheets and labels. For qualified users; it does not replace training, analysis or a real test print.

CVE-Agent

Prioritize CVE risks locally · macOS

Brings public vulnerability data from NVD, CISA KEV, FIRST EPSS and CVE List V5 together in one local Mac app. Filter by your vendor portfolio, see CVSS, EPSS and KEV signals side by side, and let the optional hourly background check notify you about new critical findings. Reports and configuration stay on your Mac.
